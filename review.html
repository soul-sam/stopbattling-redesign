v2 · the six changes

What changed, and why

Header · now

Seven years handing out the medicine. Thirty hiding behind it. / You’re writing a redemption story.

Header · suggested

You’re not stuck. You’re writing a redemption story. / Seven years serving the medicine in ceremony. Thirty years hiding behind it. I turned around.

Lead with the visitor, follow with you. "You’re not stuck" is your /long headline — it gives the stranger the contrast before the promise. "In ceremony" is one word of context. "I turned around" is your line, and it closes the arc: served, hid, turned.

Now

Four acts. You’re in one.

Suggested

Every redemption story has four acts. Which one are you in?

Keep "acts" — the word isn’t the problem, the missing frame is. Say "story" and acts explains itself.

Now

A short morning email + the guide, instantly.

Suggested

Seven mornings, one short email each. The Stop Battling guide lands in your inbox right away.

"The guide" was unnamed. Now it has a name and the reader knows the shape of the seven days.

Now

Raw daily reminders. To keep this my full-time work…

Suggested

Free, unscripted videos every morning. To keep this my full-time work…

"Reminders" is what you call the videos. A stranger doesn’t know they exist yet.

Now

A personal reading

Suggested

A personal session

To a stranger, "reading" sounds like tarot. Your own schema calls it a session. If you love "reading", keep it and let the subline carry it: "Bring your story. I’ll read it back to you."

Now

Rates are local & flexible.

Suggested

Rates are flexible — pay what makes sense where you live.

"Local" needed three reads. This says the sliding scale out loud.
